## What the desk covers

- **Domain and DNS.** Search availability and price, register, set nameservers, MX, forwarding, and privacy. `.com`, `.net`, and `.org` are typically $10–$20 a year; newer extensions and premium names cost more. A domain can be held before a site exists.
- **Custom web design.** Landing pages, portfolios, service sites, small shops, and internal tools. Mobile-first layouts, performance, accessibility, and basic SEO. Not a generic template drop.
- **Static-site SEO on Cloudflare.** [Meta Generator](https://meta-gen.registermysite.com), [Schema Generator](https://schema-gen.registermysite.com), OG preview and images. HTML Studio deploys write `robots.txt`, `llms.txt`, and `sitemap.xml`.
- **Business email.** HTML campaigns with links, images, buttons, and dark-mode-safe markup, plus open and click tracking. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC so mail does not bounce into the void.
- **Local listings.** [ListingForge](https://listingforge.registermysite.com) and the [Listing Claim Finder](https://listings.registermysite.com) for Google Business and Yelp.
- **In person.** Discovery, design review, staff training, and small-office help across Los Angeles County and Orange County: downtown LA, the Westside, South Bay, the Valley, Long Beach, Anaheim, Irvine, Santa Ana, Huntington Beach, and nearby cities. Nationwide clients stay remote.

Process: discover the goal, secure the domain and the page plan, build and preview, then launch and train. Optional care covers updates, backups, SSL, security headers, and a monthly summary.
